FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about the AI Product Management program, learning format, curriculum and certification.

Who is this program for?

The program is designed for product owners, associate product managers, product managers, business analysts, marketers, designers, developers, founders, career switchers and beginners.

Do I need a technical or coding background?

The brochure states that the program is designed to build product leadership skills without technical prerequisites.

How long is the program?

The program runs for 18 weeks, with four sessions per week.

What will I learn?

The roadmap covers core product management in Weeks 1–12 and AI Product Management in Weeks 13–18, including discovery, strategy, roadmaps, PRDs, UX, MVPs, analytics, LLMs, AI product design, RAG and AI agents.

Will I build a real product?

Yes. The Build Your Dream Product (BYDP) experience is designed around building your own product from idea to execution. BuildrX is the platform described in the brochure for hosting and showcasing the product.

Which tools are covered?

The brochure lists Jira, Asana, Notion, Confluence, Figma, Miro, Mixpanel, Google Analytics, Postman, GitHub, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Hugging Face, LangChain and LangGraph.

Who are the mentors?

The brochure features Ruchir Rana, Subhasis Chandra, Nitya Sagar and Ravi Ahlawat. Confirm active-cohort mentor availability with the admissions team.

What certification is included?

The pricing reference supplied shows an AcceleratorX + IBM Advanced AI Product Management Program and includes IBM Industry Certification. Confirm the exact credential title and completion requirements with admissions.

What is the program fee?

The supplied pricing screenshot lists the AcceleratorX + IBM Advanced AI Product Management Program at ₹59,999 + GST. Confirm current cohort pricing and availability with admissions.
